Metadata Scrubber

Strip Content Credentials, XMP, EXIF, dates, location and encoder tags from video and images. Picture and sound are copied as-is with no re-encoding.

  • MP4 · MOV · M4V
  • JPEG · PNG
  • Runs in your browser, files never upload

What it can't remove: invisible watermarks embedded in the pixels or audio themselves (e.g. SynthID or similar). Some AI tools add these, and platforms can still detect them.

Not supported yet: fragmented MP4 (some live-stream and DASH exports) and DRM-protected files.
